FAQ

Questions clients ask before starting.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

Can you take over an existing system?

Yes. Most of our work starts with a system that already exists. We begin with an audit, stabilise what is risky, add visibility, and then improve it step by step without a big-bang rewrite.

Which cloud provider should we use?

The one that fits your team, budget and compliance needs. We work across AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, and we will tell you honestly when a simpler setup is enough.

Do we own the code and the infrastructure?

Completely. Code, Terraform, pipelines and dashboards live in your accounts and repositories from day one. You can continue without us at any time.

Do you work with startups and small teams?

Yes. We help early teams build a solid foundation without over-engineering, and we size the platform to your stage, not to a hypothetical future.

What does observability actually give us?

Answers. When something breaks or slows down you see where and why in minutes, instead of guessing. It also shows which parts of the system cost the most and which matter most to users.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing reliability and support on a monthly basis, or a documented handover so your own team can run everything confidently.
